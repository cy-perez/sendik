2. What Sendik is and what it is not

Sendik is a contact portal: a platform that connects people who sell with people who buy, so that the consumer relationship is entered into directly between them.

This has a consequence worth understanding before you buy or sell: the seller sells the product, not Sendik. Sendik does not own the products listed, does not hold them, does not ship them and does not intervene in what gets listed beyond the moderation described in section 7.

What Sendik does do, and is answerable for:

  1. Verify the identity of every seller before allowing them to list (section 6).
  2. Review every listing before it becomes visible (section 7).
  3. Hold the payment until delivery is confirmed, and return it where applicable, under the Sendik Guarantee in section 13.
  4. Handle requests, complaints and claims through the channels in section 18.

The obligations Colombian law imposes on Sendik as a contact portal are met in full and are not limited by this document.

6. Seller verification

Nobody lists without being verified. Selling requires providing an identity document, a photograph of your face taken at that moment, and the bank account where payments will be received.

  1. These details are used solely to confirm that the seller is who they claim to be and to pay them. They are not shown to buyers: only the last four digits of the bank account and of the document are visible, and the facial photograph is not visible to anyone outside the review.
  2. The review is carried out by a member of the Sendik team and may be approved or rejected with a stated reason. A rejection may be corrected and resubmitted.
  3. The verified seller badge indicates that this review was passed. It is not a Sendik guarantee about the product or about the seller's performance, and does not replace the Guarantee in section 13.
  4. Sendik may revoke a seller's verification where grounds arise, informing them of the reason.

13. The Sendik Guarantee

The Guarantee is what makes buying from a stranger reasonable. It means that Sendik holds the buyer's money and does not release it to the seller until delivery is confirmed.

13.1 How it works

  1. You pay and Sendik holds the amount.
  2. The seller dispatches and the courier delivers.
  3. From delivery, you have three (3) business days —the claim window— to confirm that everything is in order or to report a non-conforming product.
  4. If you confirm, or if the window passes without confirmation or report, delivery is deemed confirmed.
  5. The payment is not handed to the seller before the five (5) business days of the right of withdrawal have elapsed, even where delivery is already confirmed. That is what ensures that if you withdraw on the fourth day, the money is still where it needs to be to return it to you.
  6. An open report, or a withdrawal exercised, suspends release of the payment until it is resolved.

13.2 What happens if the report is upheld

You are refunded the value of the product and the shipping you paid. Return freight is borne by the seller. Sendik's commission is not charged on a refunded order. The refund comes out of the held funds, so it does not depend on the seller's cooperation.

13.3 What counts as a non-conforming product

A product is non-conforming where it:

  • Does not match the listing: different model, declared size, measurements, condition or brand.
  • Arrives with damage that was not declared in the listing.
  • In technology, arrives opened having been declared sealed.

A product is not non-conforming because the size does not fit as expected, because the colour looks different on screen, or because you simply changed your mind. The right of withdrawal in section 14 covers that, and it is a different thing.

13.4 How to report

A report is opened from the order, in your account, and requires photographs of what you received. This is the only channel: a complaint by email or social media will be answered by telling you where to open it, so that there is a record. One order allows one report.

The Guarantee is a contractual undertaking Sendik offers in addition to your legal rights. It neither replaces nor limits them.

14. Right of withdrawal

As this is a distance sale, you have the right to withdraw within five (5) business days following delivery of the product, without having to give a reason.

  1. To exercise it, write to soporte@sendik.co within the period and return the product in the same condition in which you received it.
  2. Return transport costs are borne by you, unless the product is non-conforming, in which case section 13.2 applies.
  3. Once the product is received and you have supplied the correct and complete details we request for the refund, the money will be returned within a maximum of fifteen (15) calendar days from the date you exercised the right.
  4. The refund is applied to the same payment instrument you used, or by the means agreed with you. Before refunding, the available options are explained to you clearly and in detail.
  5. No deductions or withholdings of any kind are made from the amount returned.

15. Warranties

15.1 Statutory warranty

Every product sold on Sendik carries the statutory warranty established by Colombian law: that the product works for what it was offered for and matches what was advertised. This warranty is enforceable against the seller and, where the law so provides, against the producer as well.

Unless a different term is stated, the statutory warranty for a new product is one (1) year from delivery. For second-hand products the term is [[WARRANTY TERM FOR USED GOODS, TO BE CONFIRMED WITH LAWYER]].

15.2 Manufacturer warranty on technology

Where a technology product carries a manufacturer warranty, the seller declares it in the listing and states for how many months. The seller answers for that warranty, not Sendik.

Sendik offers the Guarantee in section 13 and nothing more. The statutory warranty exists in addition to all of the above and is not replaced by any rule in this document.

15.3 How to claim

16. Payment reversal

Payment reversal is different from withdrawal and from the Guarantee. It applies, among other cases, where there was fraud, where the product was not delivered, where it does not match what was ordered, or where it is defective.

To request it you must submit the request to Sendik and also to the issuer of your payment method, within the statutory time limits. Sendik is required to facilitate the process and does so.
