2. Who this applies to

It applies to the processing of personal data of:

  • Anyone who visits the site without an account.
  • Anyone who contacts us through the support channels.

3. Definitions

  • Data subject: the individual the data relates to.
  • Controller: who decides on the database and the processing. In this policy, Sendik.
  • Processor: who processes data on the controller's behalf.
  • Processing: any operation on personal data: collecting, storing, using, circulating or deleting.
  • Sensitive data: data affecting privacy or whose misuse could lead to discrimination. Includes biometric data.
  • Authorisation: the data subject's prior, express and informed consent.

4. Principles

Sendik applies the principles of the Colombian regime. In particular:

  • Purpose: every data item is collected for a specific, already defined use. If a field cannot answer what it is for, how long it is kept and who reads it, it is not created.
  • Freedom: data is processed with your authorisation, except where the law does not require it.
  • Accuracy: data must be true and up to date. You can correct it from your profile.
  • Restricted access and circulation: sensitive data is accessible only to those who must review it, and every access is logged.
  • Security and confidentiality: see section 11.

5. What data we collect

5.1 From anyone with an account

  • Identification and contact: name, email address and password. The password is stored using a hashing algorithm and nobody at Sendik can read it.
  • Optional profile data: phone, city and profile photograph. These start empty, are not needed to hold an account, and you can remove them at any time.
  • Usage data: listings marked as favourites, order history, open sessions.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and the date and time of certain actions.
  • Consent evidence: the version of the documents you accepted, the date, the time and the IP address.

5.2 Additional data from sellers

Requested only from those who choose to sell, at the time of verification:

  • Identity document number and type, and its image.
  • A photograph of the face taken at that moment, to confirm that the person verifying is the document holder.
  • Bank account number and institution, in order to pay them for their sales.

5.3 From buyers

  • Delivery address and contact details for dispatch.
  • Transaction data. Sendik does not store card numbers: that data is received and processed directly by the payment gateway.

6. Sensitive data

Sendik treats a seller's identity document number, its image and the photograph of their face as sensitive data.

  1. You are not obliged to provide sensitive data. You can hold an account, browse, buy and use the platform without providing it. It is requested only if you decide to sell, because without verifying who sells, the guarantee would mean nothing.
  2. The purpose is single: to confirm the seller's identity and prevent fraud and impersonation. It is not used for anything else, is not transferred for commercial purposes and is not shown to other users.
  3. It is held in private, encrypted storage and served only through short-lived links to the person carrying out the review.
  4. Once verification is approved, this data no longer travels to the application: the status and the date are enough.
  5. The bank account number is encrypted and only the last four digits are shown on screen.

Each purpose is independent. These are all of them:

  1. Creating and administering your account, and verifying your email address.
  2. Authenticating you and keeping your session secure.
  3. Verifying seller identity and preventing fraud and impersonation.
  4. Publishing and moderating listings.
  5. Processing orders, charging, holding the payment and paying the seller.

  7. Handling claims, warranties, withdrawals and returns.
  8. Sending you the emails necessary to operate: account verification, password reset, security notices, moderation decisions and order status. These are not advertising and cannot be opted out of while you hold an account.
  9. Handling your requests, complaints and claims.
  10. Complying with legal, accounting and tax obligations, and responding to requests from competent authorities.
  11. Maintaining the security of the service and diagnosing faults.

9. Closing your account

You can close your account from your profile. When you do:

  • Favourites are deleted, not anonymised: there is nothing left worth keeping.
  • Identity documents, the facial photograph and the bank account are removed from storage.
  • Orders and invoices are retained under accounting and tax obligations, unlinked from your profile.

11. International transfer and transmission

Your data is stored and processed outside Colombia. Sendik's infrastructure is hosted in the United States and several of the processors in section 10 operate from there.

By authorising the processing, you authorise this international transmission, which is carried out under contractual confidentiality and security commitments with each processor.

Colombian law distinguishes two figures. There is transfer where the recipient processes the data as a controller in its own right, and transmission where a processor handles it on the controller's behalf and on its instructions (Decree 1377 of 2013, incorporated into Single Decree 1074 of 2015). The providers in section 10 process your data on Sendik's behalf: the figure that applies is transmission.

That transmission requires no additional authorisation from you where there is a contract between Sendik and each processor setting out the scope of the processing, the activities the processor carries out on Sendik's behalf and its obligations towards you. Sendik only uses processors with which such a contract exists.

As to the destination country: the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce included the United States of America among the countries with an adequate level of personal data protection, so sending data there requires no further filing or declaration with that authority.

12. Security

Measures adopted, described without detail that would itself be a risk:

  • Passwords are stored with a resistant hashing algorithm and are not reversible.
  • Identity documents and facial photographs go to private, never public, storage and are served only through short-lived links.
  • Bank account numbers are encrypted in the database.
  • System logs never contain passwords, tokens, document numbers, bank accounts or facial photographs. Not even partially.
  • Service responses return only the fields the screen needs: a public profile does not include email or phone.
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted to those who must review it and is logged.
  • Development environments never receive real personal data.

No measure is infallible. Should an incident affecting your data occur, you will be informed and it will be reported to the authority in accordance with the rules in force.

13. How long we keep data

  • Account data: for as long as the account exists.
  • Identity document, facial photograph and bank account: while verification is in force. Deleted when the account is closed.
  • Orders and invoices: for the period required by accounting and tax obligations, which is ten (10) years.
  • Consent evidence: for as long as it may be needed to prove the authorisation.
  • Technical logs: thirty (30) days.

14. Minors

Sendik is not directed at minors and accounts for people under eighteen are not permitted. If we detect one, it is closed and its data deleted.

15. National Database Registry

Sendik is not required to register its databases with the National Database Registry of the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce. That obligation falls on companies and non-profit entities whose assets exceed a given threshold, and on public legal entities. Sendik is operated by an individual.

Not being required to register does not exempt Sendik from any of the above. The data protection regime applies in full, and this policy is complied with in full.
